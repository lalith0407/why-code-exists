Bug report: reassigning a 1:1 relationship that has a unique index (foreign key on the dependent side, shadow property) to an entity that is already attached, then calling DbContext.SaveChangesAsync(), throws a unique constraint violation: duplicate key value violates unique constraint "IX_...". It works in EF Core 9, EF Core 10 and 11.0.0-preview.6, and fails in 11.0.0-preview.7. Seen on PostgreSQL (Npgsql), SQL Server and SQLite.
